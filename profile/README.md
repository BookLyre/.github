# BookLyre

**Full-cast audiobooks from the books you own, generated on your device.**

BookLyre turns DRM-free ebooks into audiobooks with a distinct AI voice for every character. Everything runs on your iPhone or iPad: no accounts, no tracking, no cloud processing.

- **BookLyre** for listeners: hear the books you own, fully voiced.
- **BookLyre Studio** for independent authors: turn your manuscript into a full-cast audiobook, record your own narration, and export it for your readers.
